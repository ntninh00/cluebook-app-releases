# Cluebook for Android

APKs of the **Cluebook** student app: today's work, homework missions, tests, exam papers, your Field Guide and words, on your phone. Sign in with the account your teacher made for you.

Download the newest `cluebook-*.apk` under [Releases](../../releases) and open it on your phone. The app finds newer versions itself and offers them at the top of the screen.
